## K16 conflict note

"Any missing transition on appear/disappear is blocking" (K16) collides with two existing
`blocking` rules that require the *opposite* — an instant snap — on two specific action classes:

- the frequency gate (lens 09 · Frequency gate): 100+ times a day, no animation;
- lens 09 · Keyboard-initiated actions never animate: instant state change, always.

Resolved in this pass by writing both as explicit exceptions inside "Motion gaps" itself, so the
entry reads: any appear/disappear with no transition is blocking, *except* keyboard-initiated
and top-frequency-tier actions, where the snap is required, not a gap. This keeps all three rules
individually true with no contradiction, but it is a judgement call about how the exception is
worded, not something the owner explicitly dictated — **conflict resolved by keeping the
keyboard/frequency exception — owner to confirm.**
