Targets at least 24 × 24 px, 44 × 44 on touch · blocking

design-critic · 07 · a diagnosis with a cause, not an opinion

The critic works like a doctor. The examination is the screen card. The tests are the lenses, each checking its own thing. The diagnosis is a finding with a rule and a cause from perception science. The prescription is what to fix, cheapest first. And the product's decisions log is the medical record: the next examination remembers past diagnoses.
